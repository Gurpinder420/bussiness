Buying guide

Not sure where to start?

Start with one jar of ghee and one of honey. Both keep for months unopened, both work in everyday cooking, and both tell you quickly whether the source suits you.

  • For cooking: bilona ghee, Byadgi chilli and coriander powder.
  • For mornings: forest honey, amla and moringa powders.
  • For gifting: the festive gift box, boxed and ready to ship.
