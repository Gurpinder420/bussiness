2. What this demo actually stores

This build has no server and no database. Everything the site remembers is kept in your own browser:

  • Cart contents and the applied coupon code, in browser localStorage.
  • Wishlist items, in browser localStorage.
  • Chosen display currency, in browser localStorage.
  • Orders placed through the demo checkout, in browser localStorage on this device.

Clearing your browser storage deletes all of it. None of it is transmitted to us, because no backend is connected.

3. Information a live store would collect

  • Name, email, mobile number and delivery address, to fulfil an order.
  • Order history and payment status. Card data should never touch your servers — it belongs to the payment gateway.
  • Basic technical data (page requested, approximate region, device type) for security and performance.
  • Messages you send through the contact form or by email.

4. Why we would use it

  • To pack and deliver your order, and to handle returns.
  • To answer your questions and provide support.
  • To keep records required by tax and accounting law.
  • To send a newsletter, only if you subscribe and only until you unsubscribe.

5. Cookies and third parties

This build sets no tracking cookies. A live store would typically use a payment gateway, an email or messaging service, a courier and analytics — each receiving only the data needed for its job. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and placeholder images from placehold.co, so those services see your IP address when a page loads. Replace both with self-hosted assets if you need to avoid that.

6. Exchange-rate requests

If you switch the display currency away from INR, the site requests a public exchange-rate feed (open.er-api.com). That request does not send your cart, name or address — it only asks for current rates.

7. How long data is kept

Browser storage stays until you clear it. A live store would keep order records for as long as tax law requires, and support messages for a limited period stated in its internal retention policy.

8. Your choices

  • Clear browser storage to remove your cart, wishlist and demo orders.
  • Ask for a copy, correction or deletion of personal data a live store holds about you.
  • Unsubscribe from any newsletter using the link in the email.

9. Children

The store is not intended for children and does not knowingly collect their data.
