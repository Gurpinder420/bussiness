FAQ

Answers to the usual questions

How long does delivery take?

Orders are packed in 2–3 working days. Transit time depends on your pincode and courier. Live delivery dates need a courier API, which this demo does not have.

Can I pay online?

Not yet. No payment gateway is connected, so only Cash on Delivery is available. Card, UPI and wallet payment stay disabled until Razorpay or Stripe is set up on a server.

Are the reviews on this site real?

No. They are sample text written for the layout. This store shows no star ratings because it has no verified reviews yet.

How do I return a jar?

Unopened jars can be returned within 7 days of delivery. The refund policy page has the full starter text, which a legal professional should review.

Is the honey heated or processed?

The product copy says the honey is strained and not boiled. That is a description of the process, not a certification — confirm it with your supplier before publishing.
