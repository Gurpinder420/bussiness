3. Dispatch and delivery

  • Orders are packed within 2–3 working days of payment confirmation or COD confirmation.
  • Transit time depends on your pincode and courier — typically 3–7 working days for metros, longer for remote areas.
  • Delivery dates shown anywhere on this site are indicative, not guaranteed. This demo has no courier API, so it cannot show live dates at all.

4. Packaging

Ghee and honey travel in glass. Jars are wrapped and boxed in rigid cartons with cushioning. If a carton arrives visibly damaged, photograph it before opening and mention it to the courier.

5. Cash on delivery

COD is available across India, subject to courier serviceability at your pincode. Some couriers restrict COD for high-value orders; in that case we will contact you before dispatch.

6. Address and contact issues

  • Please give a complete address with a landmark and a reachable mobile number.
  • Repeated failed delivery attempts may lead to the parcel returning to us; return shipping may be deducted from the refund.
  • Tell us about a wrong address as soon as possible, before dispatch.

7. Lost or delayed parcels

If tracking shows no movement for 7 days, write to us with the order number and we will raise a trace with the courier. Replacement or refund follows the courier's outcome.
